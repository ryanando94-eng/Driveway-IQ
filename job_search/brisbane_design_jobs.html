Get new ads emailed to you every morning (5 minutes)
  1. SEEK: open the SEEK link in the “All roles” row, sign in, then choose Save search and set alerts to daily.
  2. LinkedIn: open the LinkedIn link and switch on Set alert above the results. Also tick “Open to work” (recruiters only) in your profile.
  3. Indeed: open the Indeed link and accept the Get new jobs for this search by email prompt.
  4. Builders: many volume builders hire through their own careers pages and LinkedIn first. Follow the builders on the Employers sheet.
  5. Recruiters: send your CV and portfolio to two or three of the specialist recruiters on the Employers sheet. Many design-manager roles are filled before they are advertised.
